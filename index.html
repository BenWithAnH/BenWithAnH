  • Async ETL Pipeline: Ingests 107K+ race results across 1,000+ meets with Jsoup and CompletableFuture, enforcing rate limiting, retries, and row-level reconciliation (99.7% balance rate).

Architecture & Technical Notes

Statistical Normalization: Computes meet-specific Course Adjustment Factors (CAF) by anchoring repeat runner performances with trimmed-mean ratios and shrinking noisy small-sample meets toward a global prior via Empirical Bayes.

Simulation & Graph Search: Runs 1,000 Monte Carlo team simulations per matchup using athlete rating distributions and official NCAA displacement tiebreakers; uses breadth-first traversal over directed head-to-head graphs to surface transitive victory chains.

Resource-Constrained Engineering: Profiled and tuned specifically for a 512 MB RAM / 0.1 vCPU deployment envelope using JDBC batching, bounded HikariCP connection pools, and compact in-memory adjacency structures.

Quantitative pricing and signal-processing engine comparing market-implied event probabilities across equity options, Fed Funds futures, and prediction markets.

  • Cross-Asset Pipeline: Aggregates and compares implied probabilities across US equity options, CME 30-Day Fed Funds futures (ZQ), and Polymarket ahead of major macro events.
  • De-Americanization & Smile Fitting: Strips early-exercise premiums with a memory-efficient 1D dynamic binomial tree and fits a 5-parameter raw SVI smile + Breeden-Litzenberger differentiation for continuous risk-neutral densities.
  • FFT Event Deconvolution: Isolates high-impact FOMC event spikes from daily background noise using an in-place Radix-2 Cooley-Tukey FFT.
  • Real-Time Tooling: Automated multi-asset ingestion and comparative visualization in Java Swing and Python.
Architecture & Technical Notes

De-Americanization (CRR.java): Uses a rolling 1D backward-induction Cox-Ross-Rubinstein binomial tree to eliminate American early-exercise premia from equity option chains prior to surface fitting.

Implied Density Extraction (BS.java, BL.java): Calibrates 5-parameter Stochastic Volatility Inspired (SVI) smiles in total-variance space and applies Breeden-Litzenberger second-order strike differentiation (f_Q(K) = e^(rT) · ∂²C/∂K²) to extract continuous risk-neutral densities.

FFT Jump Deconvolution: Isolates pure macro-announcement jump distributions by dividing pre- and post-event characteristic functions in the frequency domain via an in-place Radix-2 Cooley-Tukey FFT.
